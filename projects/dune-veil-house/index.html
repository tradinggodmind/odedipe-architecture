Dune Veil House

Comporta, PortugalSingle-storey coastal house · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Comporta, Portugal
Typology
Single-storey coastal house
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 15

A white shell rises from one side of the dune and lifts along the sea-facing veranda. An off-centre oculus brings a protected garden into the plan. Living spaces line the long glazed lip; bedrooms open from the sheltered landward hall.

A sheltered arrival court separates the bedroom wing from the sea-facing living room. The kitchen and utility open to a service yard on the landward side.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Cork-faced storage doors, pale sanded ash, sea-green lacquer and slung natural linen. The fixed daybed fits the long crescent window; small bronze pulls are recessed into the cork. Lime floors are softly burnished. Cork cabinet doors have recessed bronze pulls; a ventilated plinth holds them above the floor. A fixed ash daybed follows the crescent window, leaving the planted court as the room’s centre.
