Treecrown Residence

Karuizawa, Nagano, JapanForest mansion villa · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Karuizawa, Nagano, Japan
Typology
Forest mansion villa
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 08

The family occupies a low glazed wing embedded in the forest floor. Two inclined concrete stems carry a pair of glazed capsules above the trees. One capsule holds the bedroom, the other a reading room. The lift slit between the stems makes the ascent visible from outside.

The low family wing sits against the slope. A lift and enclosed stair within the mast serve a high, private sleeping and reading floor.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Ivory mineral concrete, blackened elm casework, brushed pewter pulls and moss-green wool. Shoji-like vertical screens sit inside insulated glass; the crown has a fixed elm bed ledge and a single paper reading lamp. A blackened-elm bed ledge follows the crown window. An independent interior screen can close for privacy without changing the silhouette of the glazed capsule.
