Crescent Current Centre

Jabi Lake, Abuja, NigeriaPerformance and dance centre · Concept visualisation · 2026

Typology

Perspective & drawing studies

2026 · Study 01

An ivory plaza rises into two crests at the edge of Jabi Lake. The lower crest opens around the public foyer. The taller one contains the recital hall. A slim planted cut brings daylight between the two and gives the dance studio its own lakeside address.

A visitor ascends the broad public apron into the lake foyer, then turns inland for the recital hall. Workshops and backstage rooms have an uphill service route.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Flamed pale granite runs from plaza to foyer. An iroko handrail returns into an indigo leather bench. Inside the recital room, narrow smoked-iroko ribs wrap the walls and acoustic canopy, with wine-red upholstered seats. The iroko handrail turns into the back of the lake bench. A finger joint separates the straight rail from its radius. The indigo seat is removable, so it can be re-covered independently of the timber.
