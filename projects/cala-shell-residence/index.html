Cala Shell Residence

Luštica Peninsula, MontenegroCoastal mansion villa · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Luštica Peninsula, Montenegro
Typology
Coastal mansion villa
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 07

The roof wraps a private pool court in a single almond-shaped contour. Its upper lip lifts to give the salon shade and an open sea view. The landward shell thickens into the entry cleft and protects the upper bedrooms from the arrival path.

An uphill cleft leads into the upper sleeping wing; a stair curls down to the sheltered salon and pool terrace. A service passage follows the thick landward flank.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Pearl-toned exterior, pale bush-hammered limestone on the terrace, waxed walnut inside and sea-green linen. A curved walnut banquette sits at the sheltered end of the pool court; its cushion seams follow the aperture. The curved walnut banquette is built in segments; cushion seams stop at the panel joints. A pale stone curb distinguishes the pool from the living floor even when both surfaces are wet.
