Split Ribbon Duplex

Ikoyi, Lagos, NigeriaPaired two-storey homes · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Ikoyi, Lagos, Nigeria
Typology
Paired two-storey homes
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 10

A continuous white ribbon rises over two independent front doors, dips at the party wall and lifts again over the second home. From the garden, two separate patios make that division equally clear. Each household has two upstairs bedrooms and its own stair.

Each home has its own street door, stair, kitchen, two upper bedrooms and private rear garden. The shared dip in the roof marks the party line from the street.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Brass gauze, iroko ceiling battens and indigo strip-woven aso-oke upholstery. Honey-toned terrazzo floors wrap the stairs. One walnut settle fits each ribbon's concave wall; each home has its own garden. Indigo strip-woven panels run across a removable settle back. Each textile width is visible at its stitched seam; iroko battens continue the ceiling rhythm through the shaded rear patio.
