Strata Prow Villa

Hout Bay, Cape Town, South AfricaHillside mansion villa · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Hout Bay, Cape Town, South Africa
Typology
Hillside mansion villa
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 09

A thin roof projects above a thicker triangular terrace. Between them, a long pool narrows toward the prow, and the living rooms open along the sheltered side. Only the inland windbreak curves; the roof and pool keep their sharp edges.

The upper entry opens onto the short end of the wedge. Living and dining share its long sea edge; bedrooms sit below, with a protected service route on the uphill side.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Shell-white fair-faced concrete, honed charcoal quartzite, weathered bronze and oxblood saddle leather. A tapered walnut dining table meets the pool-facing wall with a concealed expansion joint. A tapered walnut dining table echoes the prow in plan. Its hidden expansion joint permits seasonal movement while a straight sightline remains open to the headland.
