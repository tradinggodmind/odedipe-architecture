Stream Ledge House

Hudson Valley near Woodstock, New York, USAWoodland house and writing room · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Hudson Valley near Woodstock, New York, USA
Typology
Woodland house and writing room
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 14

A fieldstone chimney anchors two concrete terrace trays above the rocky stream. An entry bridge reaches the stone spine from uphill. The upper writing room looks back through the canopy, while the lower salon follows the water horizontally.

A footbridge reaches the stone spine. The living area turns toward the stream, with two quiet rooms upstairs and a writing ledge over the lower terrace.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Flagstone floors bend around a hearth boulder. Cherry built-ins follow the slab edges, lichen-green wool covers a window seat, and a shallow aged-copper hearth hood catches reflected water light. Waxed cherry shelves terminate against the stone with a narrow movement gap. Lichen wool softens the stream-facing window seat while a copper hood reflects light back into the hearth.
