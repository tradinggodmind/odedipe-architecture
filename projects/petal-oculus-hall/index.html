Petal Oculus Hall

Sabarmati riverfront, Ahmedabad, IndiaChamber-music hall · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Sabarmati riverfront, Ahmedabad, India
Typology
Chamber-music hall
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 03

Nine radial bays gather around the recital chamber. Outer petals shelter people at the water courts; inner petals rise around the stage. The widened west bay opens a deliberate view of the Sabarmati and gives the hall a different front from its symmetrical precedent.

Visitors cross a shallow radial water court into the circular recital chamber. A quiet perimeter gallery separates arriving audiences from backstage preparation.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Pale Makrana-type marble, warm sandstone plinths, limewashed inner ribs, teak armrests, and dark saffron wool cushions. Bronze mesh diffuses the daylight between two petals instead of covering the whole ceiling. Teak armrests soften the edge of the stone seating plinth. Bronze mesh above the aisles filters shafts of light; dark saffron seat backs absorb light near the stage.
