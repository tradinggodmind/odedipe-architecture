Tidefold Tower

West Kowloon, Hong KongOffice and events tower · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
West Kowloon, Hong Kong
Typology
Office and events tower
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 06

The waterfront podium turns into four rounded glass lobes. The twist eases as the tower rises; one deeply recessed sky garden marks the upper floors. A transparent crown puts the ballroom, rather than plant or signage, at the top of the public silhouette.

The street lobby joins the waterfront promenade. Upper offices surround a fixed core. A planted terrace occupies L27 and the ballroom occupies the crown.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Pale celadon glass, satin nickel, cream limestone and fluted cast-glass screens. Dark mulberry upholstery on crescent benches makes the rounded lobby legible; the crown ballroom has a finely ribbed ash floor. A mulberry crescent seat makes the lobby’s curved glass legible at body height. The ballroom floor changes direction across the lobe with thin ash strips and quiet movement joints.
