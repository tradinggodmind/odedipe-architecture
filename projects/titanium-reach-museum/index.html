Titanium Reach Museum

2026 · Study 02

Three titanium lobes turn toward the water. Two limestone bars hold rectangular galleries behind them. A bridge crosses a narrow inlet and lets a visitor see the difference between the intimate galleries and the full height of the central atrium.

The waterside bridge joins two gallery walks. Art handling and conservation run along the landward bar; visitors orient themselves from the atrium.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Bush-hammered pale stone, bead-blasted titanium balustrades and smoked-oak guardrails. The atrium has low felt-topped benches on black steel feet; galleries use a pale, quiet end-grain timber floor. The felt seat floats on a folded steel foot set in from its edge. A smoked-oak hand surface lines the guard where visitors pause above the bridge; metal is left exposed on its underside.
