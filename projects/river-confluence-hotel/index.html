River Confluence Hotel

Kallang Basin, SingaporeWaterfront city hotel · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Kallang Basin, Singapore
Typology
Waterfront city hotel
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 13

Two unequal towers share a three-level waterfront podium. The podium’s diamond panels open gradually into the tower glazing. At ground level a split river stair brings guests to the shared lobby, where each wing has its own lift bank.

The river stair leads into a shared lobby. Each accommodation wing has its own lift bank. Service kitchens and laundry enter through the city side of the podium.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Silver-grey diamond GFRC, dark grey limestone, stitched pandan-weave panels and oxblood lacquer. Over the lobby bar a pale ribbed metal canopy narrows like the podium's glazed throat; bedrooms use ventilated woven panels and oval reading lamps. Pandan-weave wall panels sit in removable cassettes behind the oxblood reception seating. The faceted canopy over the bar borrows the podium joint spacing at a smaller scale.
