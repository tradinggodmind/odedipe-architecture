Estuary Spine Tower

Five Cowries Creek, Lagos, NigeriaCreekside residential tower · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Five Cowries Creek, Lagos, Nigeria
Typology
Creekside residential tower
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 04

White perimeter branches cradle two almond-shaped openings at the base, then narrow along the glass tower. The creek garden enters between the roots. Above, the branches pass the balconies at changing angles, becoming part of the view from the apartments.

Public arrival and deliveries separate at the podium. A fixed central core serves the residences, with a shared pool and club beneath the crown.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Grey-green terrazzo with a dark stone matrix, saddle-stitched petrol-blue leather, brushed bronze reveals and fine woven palm back panels. Full-height bay glazing is set behind the white structural branches. Petrol-blue leather is saddle-stitched onto a curved walnut frame. Woven palm forms replaceable backs in the lobby, while a terrazzo skirting separates upholstery from wet shoes.
