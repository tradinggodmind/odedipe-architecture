Meridian Rift Tower

Qianhai, Shenzhen, ChinaWorkplace tower and public atrium · Concept visualisation · 2026
Setting
Qianhai, Shenzhen, China
Typology
Workplace tower and public atrium
Work
Exterior & interior visualisation
Perspective & drawing studies
Status
Unbuilt concept
Collection
2026 · Study 05

Two glazed halves turn past one another above a shared plaza. The diagonal rift remains visible all the way to the crown. Three bridges cross it at different heights so the building’s most unusual space is also part of the working day.

The plaza continues into a full-height atrium. Two independent vertical cores work on opposite sides. Enclosed bridges connect the halves at three defined levels.

Material & atmosphere

At the scale
of touch.

Blackened aluminium balcony noses, brushed bronze rails, smoke-coloured terrazzo and celadon wool. An oval reception desk is clad in vertically brushed pewter. The atrium floor and balcony edges reflect more softly than a mirror. A pewter desk has vertical brush marks that conceal small scratches. Celadon bridge seats sit low enough to keep the changing section visible from both directions.
